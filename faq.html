Questions people ask me

If you don't see your question here, just ask. I'm happy to talk it through on a free call.

Food and dietary needs

Can you cook gluten-free?

Yes. Anything marked GF on the menu is made without gluten. My kitchen work isn't certified gluten-free, so if someone in your home has celiac disease, let's talk about it before we start.

Do you cook vegetarian?

Yes. There are vegetarian choices on the menu every week, marked with a V.

Can you make my meals nut-free?

Yes, on request. Let me know on your intake form and I'll make sure your meals are nut-free. Dishes marked NF are nut-free as written.

Do you cook vegan or dairy-free?

Not right now. I want to do a great job for every client, and vegan and dairy-free cooking aren't where I shine.

Do you count macros?

No, I don't count macros or calories. I do list every ingredient for every dish, so you always know what you're eating.

What about allergies?

I list the ingredients for everything I make. My cooking isn't certified allergen-free, so if someone in your home has a serious allergy, please tell me before we start. We'll talk it through together and decide what's safe.

Ordering and scheduling

What if I forget to pick my meals?

No problem. If I don't hear from you by Friday at noon, you'll get Chef's Picks. I choose a mix of dishes from the menu based on what I know you like.

Can I pause when I'm traveling?

Of course. Just skip that week by Friday at noon. You can skip as many weeks as you need, and there's no fee.

Is there a contract or subscription?

No. You can pause or stop anytime. Just let me know by Friday at noon.

Where do you cook?

I cook in homes in Arvada and about 30 minutes around it. Travel is included in that area. If you're a little farther out, ask me anyway.

Cook day

Do I need to be home?

Nope. Lots of clients give me a door code or use a lockbox. If you'd rather be home, that's great too.

I have pets. Is that okay?

Absolutely. I love animals. Just let me know their names and anything I should know, like whether they need to stay in another room.

What do I need to have in my kitchen?

Some glass containers, a couple of baking dishes, and a cleared fridge shelf. I bring the labels. Here's the full checklist.

Meals and food safety

How long do the meals last?

Your meals are best eaten within 4 days of your cook day. That's why each week covers four days of food.

How do you keep food safe?

I follow safe food handling practices every time I cook. Food is cooled properly before it goes in the fridge, and every container is labeled with what it is and the date it was made.

Money

How do groceries work?

I shop for your meals and charge you exactly what the groceries cost. You'll get an itemized receipt after every cook day.

How do I pay?

After our free call, we'll set up your card through Square. You won't ever need to enter card details on this website.

Do you have a referral perk?

Yes! Refer a friend, and you both get a free add-on of your choice, like a quart of soup, a snack box, or dessert. Just have your friend put your name on their intake form.
