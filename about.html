My story

I fell in love with cooking about four years ago, during a hard season with my mental health. Trying new recipes and flavors gave me something to look forward to. It brought happiness back into my days.

It started with Molly Yeh's show, Girl Meets Farm. I cooked my way through most of her cookbook, Molly on the Range. Then I picked up Salt Fat Acid Heat, which taught me real technique and how to put a whole dish together. I'll never forget my first loaf of challah. It tasted so good I cried.

I'm also a licensed therapist, and I still see couples and individuals in my virtual practice. Life gets busy for all of us. My husband Ty and I have learned that in our busiest seasons, everything feels more doable when we have a plan for what we're eating.

Full Fridge Co. brings those two parts of me together. I come to your home, cook real food, and leave your fridge full. You get your evenings back, and you get to eat really well.

When I'm not cooking for clients, Ty and I are playing board games, traveling, or taking our bernedoodle Remi on an adventure. I grew up in Arizona, but our trips are what made me fall in love with food. I still think about a pineapple tomato gazpacho we had in Paris. At home, I cook a lot of pasta and bake a lot of sweets, because nothing makes a house feel cozier.
