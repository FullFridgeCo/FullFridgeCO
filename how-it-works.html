Weekly meal prep

How weekly meal prep works

You pick, I cook, and your fridge is full for four days. Here's what a normal week looks like.

Your week, step by step

  1. 1 Wednesday

    The new menu comes out

    I post the menu on my Substack, and I send you a text with your order link.

  2. 2 By Friday at noon

    Pick your meals, or skip the week

    Choose what sounds good for the week ahead. Away or just not feeling it? Tell me you're skipping and that's it.

  3. 3 Your cook day

    I shop and cook in your kitchen

    I pick up your groceries, then come to your home on your usual day. I cook everything, label it, and clean up before I go.

  4. 4 The next 4 days

    Open the fridge and eat

    Your meals are portioned and labeled, so you know what each one is and how to warm it up. They cover the next four days.

Too busy to pick? I've got you.

If I don't hear from you by Friday at noon, you'll get Chef's Picks. That's a mix of dishes from the menu that I choose with your tastes in mind. You never end up with an empty fridge.

A few things to know

Same day every week

We pick one day that works for your household, and that becomes your cook day. It makes the week feel easy and predictable.

No subscription

There's no contract. Pause for a trip, skip a busy week, or stop anytime. Just let me know by Friday at noon.

Your containers, my labels

I cook into your own glass containers and bring the labels. Here's what to have on hand.
