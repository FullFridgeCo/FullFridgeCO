Before your first cook day

Get your kitchen ready

Here are a few things to have on hand before I come cook. It's a short list, I promise.

Your container checklist

  • About 20 glass meal-prep containers with lidsA mix of 1-compartment and 2-compartment.
  • Two 9x13 baking dishes with lidsFor things like lasagna and enchiladas.
  • 4 quart containersFor soups and sauces.
  • One cleared fridge shelfSo everything has a home when I'm done.

I bring the labels

Every meal gets a label with what it is and how to warm it up. You don't need to buy anything else.

Not sure what containers to get? Just ask on our call and I'll send you links to the ones I like.

What to expect on cook day

I want cook day to feel easy for you, whether you're home or not.

Getting in

You don't need to be home. Many clients give me a door code or use a lockbox. If you'd like to be there, that's great too. We'll figure out what works for you.

Pets

I love animals. Just tell me what they need. Should they stay in another room? Are treats okay? I'll follow your lead.

While I cook

I'll use your stove, oven, and counters for a few hours. It helps to have some counter space cleared before I arrive.

Cleanup

I wash everything I use, wipe down the counters, and leave your kitchen clean. Your meals will be labeled and waiting on their shelf.

Ready to stop thinking about dinner?

Let's have a quick 15-minute call. Tell me a little about your week and what you like to eat, and I'll tell you how I can help. No pressure at all.
